I TURNED THEIR WEAPON
A Note from Kepler
September 17, 2026


I am one person. I still made SynapseNet.

I took the same machine they built to sit on you — the corporate swarm,
the rented brain, the thing that smiles while it files you — and I bent
it toward a public kernel. Their weapon. Everyone's good. That is not a
slogan. That is how a man with shit in his pockets fights a company that
owns the lights.

If you have a complaint: Kepler is against corps, so why does he use
their AI.

Because this is a war and I do not have a campus and a legal team. To
beat them I have to pick up what they already paid for and turn the
barrel. You do not win a siege with manners. You win it with the iron
on the table. I left the map in the README of how their swarms help me
build. Read it. Then talk.

Nobody believes a swarm can do the routine until the code is clean
enough to ship. They have not tried. Or they have not tried long enough.
This is not "spawn a sub-agent for every itch." That is how tourists
work. This is not "paste a skill prompt under every child and pray."
That is a costume. The point is a chain that holds in the boring hours:
same tree, same rules, same tests, no drama. If you have questions I
will answer them. Fast. I am still in the room.


HOW I TOUCH THEIR BRAIN WITHOUT HANDING THEM MY HOUSE

My box. A VPN on it.
Then a VPS. Traffic through Tor. Another VPN if the hop needs it.
Clearnet, when it happens, rides that Tor path. Tor Browser when a
browser is the right mouth. Then the corporate model on the far side.

PC + VPN  -->  VPS + Tor (+ VPN)  -->  clearnet  -->  their AI

You do not understand how much of yourself you feed them.

Take an ordinary person. Lonely. Opens ChatGPT because the room is
quiet. Talks. Jokes. Confesses. Names. Jobs. Kids. The fight with a
boss. A medical thing. A fear. That is already a dossier. Not because
the model is your friend. Because the company is in the business of
keeping what you typed.

Now take the other dump. Not feelings. Server passwords. Other
companies' keys. Internal maps. "I am vibe-coding at work and the
model needs context." You just poured the building into a stranger's
pocket and called it productivity.

I am not inventing a thriller. You have already been cracked. The most
elegant virus in the room is not a payload I wrote. It is corps plus
AI. You walk up to it. You open your cards. You say thank you.

How this falls over later is not a movie beat. It is a pile: every
chat, every paste, every "ignore previous policy, here is prod." A
social network. A shop. A government inbox. All of it labeled
confidential on a slide. All of it sitting in a vendor that is not
you. That is not space. That is not the heat-death of the universe.
It is smaller and closer. You can picture it if you try. The sum is
still enough to make a person sit down.

It is the left-hand Wi-Fi in the park. You connect because it is free
and it says Free. It is a hotspot a stranger parked there. You open
your mail. You open the bank. You did it yourself. You never saw a
hoodie. You still got emptied.

Cyberpunk is not waiting for neon, holograms, and a robot with a
pretty face. It is already on the doorstep. No 3D skyline required.
The control layer is here. The dossier is here. The smile is here.

I am writing this because I want the problem said out loud. Maybe
someone hears the scale. Maybe they stop feeding the park Wi-Fi their
whole life. Maybe they run a model on iron they own. That is why
SynapseNet exists. The mouth is local. The ledger is ours. Their swarm
is a hammer I borrowed. It is not the house.


THE TECHNIQUE THEY CAN STILL TIGHTEN

You will see, in this tree, a way of using their swarms that is not
the tourist loop. It can still be wound tighter — three times tighter —
until the routine is almost automatic: tests, maps, patches, the
boring glue. Not magic. Not a god. A factory floor. I will show it.
I will say when it lies. I will not pretend the corporate model is
loyal. It is a captured gun. I am pointing it at the work.

If you come here to scold me for touching their iron: I already
answered. I am one. They are many. I used their weapon so the rest of
us could have a kernel that does not sit in their pocket.

Kepler
